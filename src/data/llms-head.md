## What I Do

I spent twenty years in tech at Nokia, Novatek and AUO: seven as an engineer, ten as a product manager, and three in data analysis as staff to a business unit head. I studied decision analysis at Minerva University (Master of Decision Analysis, 2023–2025).

In 2025 I left my company, the year Claude Code came out. I was among its first users and built products with it. Then my goals, my daily work, what I learn, my clients and my life at home moved onto it, one piece at a time. Readers asked how I work with AI, so I kept writing about that. Then people wanted to learn it and companies wanted to adopt it: I started teaching, went into companies to work with their teams, and founded Confluence Partners with a few partners.

In August 2026 I passed Anthropic's Claude Certified Architect exam (Foundations). Verify on Credly: https://www.credly.com/badges/476d2a2e-42ce-41a3-81b1-474c8750c044

Core belief: Direction over speed. Systems over goals. Set the direction first, build a system you use every day, and only then let AI be the lever.
